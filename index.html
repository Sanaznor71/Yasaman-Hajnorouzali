Background

About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. My research bridges autonomous driving, robotics, machine learning, and control theory.

I am especially interested in how models and controllers behave beyond idealized simulations, using route data, on-device computation, and experimental evaluation to improve reliability.

Technical interests: Python · C/C++ · MATLAB · ROS 2 · PyTorch · optimization · MPC · state estimation
